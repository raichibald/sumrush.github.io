Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Sum Rush app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Raitis Šaripo (collectively, the "Application"). Raitis Šaripo is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".


What stays on your device

Sum Rush is designed to work without an account. Your coins, abilities, personal bests, statistics, game history and settings are stored on your device, in the Application's own local database.

None of this content is sent to the Service Provider. There is no Sum Rush account, and the Service Provider cannot see your progress, your coins or your personal bests. The only exceptions are described in the sections below.


No accounts and no cloud sync

The Application does not ask you to register, sign in, or provide a name, email address or phone number. It has no server of its own, and your progress is not synced or backed up to the Service Provider. Your data lives on the device it was created on, and it travels with that device's own iCloud or iTunes backup if you have one, which is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Deleting the Application deletes the data it holds.


Advertising

The Application is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. Banner ads are shown on menu screens and never while you are answering puzzles. Rewarded video ads are played only when you choose one, for example to double the coins earned in a round or to continue a Sudden Death run. Ads are limited to general-audience content.

  • Google AdMob may collect your device's Internet Protocol address, device and app information, ad interactions, and — only if you allow tracking — your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA), in order to show and measure ads.
  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the Application's Settings under Privacy Options.
  • iOS asks, through App Tracking Transparency, whether ads may use your Advertising Identifier. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change this at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • The Remove Ads purchase turns off banner ads permanently. Rewarded ads remain available as an option.

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects analytics and crash information when you download and use it, in order to find bugs and understand which features are used. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address
  • The screens of the Application that you open, and when
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The mobile operating system and device model you use
  • An app instance identifier generated for analytics purposes
  • Gameplay events, such as which puzzle type, play style and difficulty you start, the result of a round (score, correct answers, longest combo), and whether an ability, a rewarded ad or an in-app purchase was used
  • Diagnostic information about a crash, if the Application crashes, including which mode was being played at the time

Analytics events and crash reports are not linked to your name, email address or any account, and the Application has no way to collect them: it never asks for them. The Application does not collect your contacts, location, photos or anything you type.


Cookies and tracking technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use cookies, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies to support functionality, analytics, advertising, or service delivery. Where required by applicable law, the Service Provider will obtain consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.

Sum Rush uses the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking through App Tracking Transparency, and only for advertising. If you do not allow it, the Application does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by the Service Provider. To exercise these rights, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, contact the Service Provider at saripo.raitis@gmail.com.

Consent for personalised ads can be withdrawn in the Application's Settings under Privacy Options, and tracking can be turned off in the iOS Settings app.

Purchases

Coin packs and Remove Ads are in-app purchases sold through Apple. Payment is handled by Apple and the Service Provider never receives or sees your payment details. Purchases are verified on your device from Apple's own signed transaction records; there is no purchase account or server held by the Service Provider, and restoring Remove Ads on another device is handled by Apple.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider retains personal data based on its necessity for the stated purposes:

  • Data required for legal compliance: Retained as long as required by applicable law

You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to any legal obligation to retain it, by contacting the Service Provider at saripo.raitis@gmail.com.
