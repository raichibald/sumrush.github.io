How it plays

Three puzzles, three ways to play.

  • 01

    Three kinds of puzzle

    Solve: type the answer to a quick sum like 7 × 8 or 36 ÷ 4. Compare: pick the sign that makes it true. Fill the Gap: find the missing numbers and signs so the equation works.

  • 02

    Three ways to play

    Beat the Clock: as many as you can in 60 seconds. Sudden Death: one mistake and it is over, and the clock gets faster as your streak grows. Endless: no clock, no score, just puzzles. Pick Easy, Medium or Hard for each.

  • 03

    Chain your combos

    Get answers right in a row and your combo climbs. Hold your nerve and watch the score jump.

  • 04

    A little help when it’s tight

    Earn coins as you play and spend them on abilities. Time Freeze stops the clock for 5 seconds, Skip moves past a stinker without breaking your combo, and Hint gives you a nudge on a Compare or Fill the Gap blank.

  • 05

    Watch yourself get quicker

    Personal bests for every mode, accuracy by puzzle type, your longest combo and your best Sudden Death streak.

  • 06

    Made to feel good

    Big, clear numbers, a satisfying number pad, gentle haptics and sounds you can switch off. Light and dark appearance.

Questions

Good questions.

Do I need an account?

No account needed. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

Which difficulties are there?

Easy, Medium and Hard, which you pick for each puzzle type and play style.

What are coins for?

You earn coins as you play and spend them on abilities: Time Freeze, Skip and Hint.

Can I turn the sound off?

Yes. The sounds can be switched off, and the app has light and dark appearance.

What does it need?

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later.
